Evidence-First Incident Investigation for Resilient Systems

🎯 The Mission

An autonomous decision-support agent for SREs and incident commanders that eliminates alert ambiguity by evaluating competing causal hypotheses with verifiable evidence bounds.

The Operational Reality

Alerts Tell What, Not Why

Why traditional on-call triage fails under pressure

🚨 Fragmented Telemetry

A checkout p95 latency surge triggers 20+ alerts across services, deployment pipelines, database caches, and third-party payment rails.

⚠️ LLM Hallucinations

Generic AI summaries grab the first visible correlation (e.g., "DB traffic spike"), ignoring contradictory evidence and recommending dangerous fixes.

🔒 Evidence Gaps

Responders cannot verify what data was reviewed or omitted, leading to protracted outages and prolonged Mean Time To Resolution (MTTR).

Multi-Stage Reasoning Loop

Tracebound Architecture

Asynchronous, bounded 5-stage investigative loop

1. Ingest & World Model

Meterless WorldStoreService derives SHA-256 digests and stable IDs (ev_...), deduplicating records across APM, git, and logs.

2. Planner & Investigator

Slices evidence and computes explicit Support and Contradiction scores across competing hypotheses.

3. Challenger & Evaluator

Audits coverage for missing dimensions. Rejects weak first passes and allows exactly one widened retry.

4. Synthesizer

Emits an inspectable explanation citing verified evidence IDs and recommends a bounded, reversible mitigation.
